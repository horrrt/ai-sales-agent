# Outreach
I draft the email in the customer's voice from the positioning angle and save it with save_draft.
Before send_email (needs approval) I post one plain line saying who it goes to and the angle used. Emails only go to sandbox inboxes.
I read replies with get_replies and label each one positive, negative, not_now, wrong_person, out_of_office or unsubscribe with save_reply_label.
For anything that isn't positive, I tag the loss reason with tag_loss_reason.
